## Deliverable 1: Category Confirmation

**Category:** 1 — Infant and Child Foods (Ages 0–5)
**Taxonomy version:** v2.0, March 30, 2026
**Exposure pathway:** Ingestion (oral)
**Target population:** Infants and children ages 0–5
**Regulatory jurisdiction:** FDA (Closer to Zero), EU (Reg. 2023/915
and amendments), Codex Alimentarius

**Base subcategory list (9 items from taxonomy):**
1. Infant formula (powder)
2. Infant formula (ready-to-feed, liquid)
3. Baby cereals / grain products (dry)
4. Fruit purées (general)
5. Non-root vegetable purées
6. Root-vegetable purées
7. Mixed meals (e.g., meat & grain combos)
8. Fruit juice (not canned)
9. Teething & snacks (rice-based and non-rice)

**Boundary rules confirmed:**
- Product must be explicitly marketed/positioned for ages 0–5 or
  otherwise intended for infant/toddler feeding
- Categorization follows food matrix/type regardless of packaging format
- A snack marketed for toddlers routes here; same formulation for
  general consumption routes to Categories 3–7

**Routing conflicts:** None identified. Product Cross-Reference Index
confirms: baby cereal → Cat 1, toddler snack (marketed 0–5) → Cat 1,
infant formula → Cat 1.
